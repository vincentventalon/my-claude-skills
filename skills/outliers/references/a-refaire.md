# The 5 to remake: the format

`OUTLIERS.md` ends its reading with five ideas to remake. Another skill (the
`script` skill of this repository, or yours) reads them without guessing.

## Where

In the newest `outliers-<date>/OUTLIERS.md`, under the heading
`## Les 5 à refaire` (French report) or `## 5 to remake` (English report),
right after the comment `<!-- a-refaire v1 ... -->`. `v1` is the version of
this format: a reader that does not find it stops and says so.

## The table

Six columns, always in this order, whatever the language of the headers:

| # | header (fr) | header (en) | holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#` | `#` | 1 to 5 |
| 2 | `sujet` | `topic` | the topic, in a few words |
| 3 | `hook d'origine` | `original hook` | the first sentence said, verbatim, in `« »` (or `""`), then the account: `« … » @an_account` |
| 4 | `pourquoi ça a marché (les chiffres)` | `why it worked (the numbers)` | numbers only: × median (`*` = under 3 days, a floor), views against the account median, repeated (with its counts) or lone, length against the account's |
| 5 | `ton angle` | `your angle` | one sentence in the user's voice: what they would say, from their own experience |
| 6 | `source` | `source` | the link to the original video |

One row, one idea, one line. No `|` inside a cell. Fewer than 5 rows is
allowed; the reason is written under the table.

A row, in a French report:

```text
| 2 | le CV refusé en 6 secondes | « Ton CV part à la poubelle en 6 secondes, et ce n'est pas ton expérience. » @an_account | ×4,2 (12 300 vues, médiane 2 900) ; répété : 3 coups sur 4 vidéos sur le CV chez 2 comptes ; 38 s contre 52 s | le CV que j'ai trié moi-même quand je recrutais, et ce qui m'a fait le garder | https://www.tiktok.com/@an_account/video/123 |
```

## Reading it

Split each row after the separator line on `|`, trim, take the cells by
position. Then:

- **the topic and the angle** are where the script starts;
- **the original hook** is a structure to learn from (a question, a number,
  "you", a warning), never words to reuse;
- **the numbers** stay with the idea: the script's notes say which video it
  comes from, and the user's version is judged at × their own median over 3
  to 5 videos. It is a test, not a promise;
- **the source** is to watch, not to copy.
